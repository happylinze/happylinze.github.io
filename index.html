About

I am a Ph.D. student at Jiangnan University, supervised by Prof. Xiao-Jun Wu. My research is on video understanding, with a focus on human action recognition: learning from few or zero labelled examples, modelling skeletons, and localizing actions in long, untrimmed videos.

I also enjoy turning research into working systems. With my teammates I have placed in the top three at challenges held with CVPR, ICCV, ECCV and ICME, and I build open-source tools such as HuggingFace-Manager.

  • Action recognition
  • Video understanding
  • Few- & zero-shot learning
  • Skeleton-based modelling
  • Temporal action localization
  • Multi-modal learning
